Kara Task Dialog Final Corrections
Base: f390e8f

Implemented
- Rearranged the shared Add/Edit form to Title, Due date, date shortcuts,
  Priority/List, Notes, and the standard bottom-right Cancel/Save actions.
- Added Today, Tomorrow, and Next week shortcuts using local calendar dates.
  Each updates the editor's shared due value and clears its validation error.
- Anchored the native calendar popover to its calendar button. Source inspection
  of the pinned libcosmic implementation showed that popup position clamps to
  the available overlay bounds and that the popover's outside-click handler
  tracks its wrapped content. The trigger now wraps only the calendar button.
  The calendar sits in a COSMIC Card container for an opaque, theme-derived
  surface; Escape closes it, and native outside-click and date-selection events
  remain connected.
- Replaced the small icon buttons with 48x48 COSMIC icon buttons containing
  centered 24px symbolic icons. The IconVertical theme class supplies the
  selected-state surface, while normal hover/focus styling, tooltips, and
  accessible names are retained.
- Added title-specific whitespace validation, inline error text, and the same
  theme-derived error border used by the due-date field. Valid title input clears
  the title error; invalid submission leaves the dialog open.
- Kept the existing manual date parser, storage format, shared Add/Edit path,
  Matrix task classification, list assignment, sidebar, titlebar, and tray
  behavior intact.

Known limitation

  the calendar is clamped to the available overlay bounds, but final placement,
  usable space, and pointer dismissal need CachyOS graphical acceptance at the
  supported window sizes.

CachyOS COSMIC graphical acceptance checklist
1. At about 1280x800, inspect Add Task and Edit Task. Confirm full-width Title,
   dedicated Due date row, Today/Tomorrow/Next week directly beneath it,
   Priority and List together on one row, full-width Notes, and bottom-right
   Cancel/Save. Confirm no internal scrollbar appears.
2. Repeat at about 800x600 and the minimum supported 640x560 size. Confirm the
   form remains readable, fields and footer are reachable, and opening the
   calendar does not clip it or extend it beyond the display.
3. Check each date shortcut in Add, Edit, and Matrix-created tasks. Confirm
   Today uses the local date, Tomorrow advances one calendar day, Next week
   advances seven days, and values format as YYYY-MM-DD. Check Dec/Jan and
   February/leap-year boundaries; confirm a previous due-date error clears.
4. Open the calendar from its icon. Confirm it is anchored to that button, has
   an opaque surface matching the active COSMIC theme, appears above dialog
   content, and stays within the available display. Navigate both directions
   without closing it, choose a day and confirm the shared field updates; then
   verify Escape, outside click, and selection dismiss it as expected. Test
   keyboard focus and date selection.
5. Confirm Low, Normal, and High show centered down-arrow, minus, and up-arrow
   icons in consistent 48x48 targets. Test mouse and keyboard hover/focus and
   clear selected-state contrast in light and dark themes. Verify tooltips and
   screen-reader names, Normal for new tasks, and the existing value on Edit.
6. Submit empty and whitespace-only titles. Confirm the title border and local
   message are visible, focus remains usable, Save does not close the dialog,
   and entering a non-whitespace title clears the error. Confirm due-date error
   border and correction behavior remain intact.
7. Create and edit Inbox, named-list, and Matrix tasks. Confirm their assignment,
   priority, due date, and Matrix quadrant/order are preserved. Verify the
   application titlebar remains KARA and page headings/sidebar are unchanged.
8. Smoke-test tray close/reopen and second-instance activation after dialog use.
